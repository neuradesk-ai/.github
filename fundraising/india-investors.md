## Watch-outs across the board

1. **Competitor adjacency risk.** Kalaari (HireBound) and Z47 (Oolka) have recent adjacent bets — scope explicit differentiation before pitching.
2. **Stage mismatch.** Lightspeed, Owl, GSV, Elevation skew Series A. Skip at pre-seed; revisit at seed+.
3. **Entity domicile.** Naval, Sahil Lavingia, parts of Z21 prefer Delaware C-Corp. Decide on India-domicile vs. flip strategy *before* engaging.
4. **Competitive landscape.** AIApply, Careerflow, Jobright, Final Round AI, HireBound, Mercor, Wonsulting are all in the conversation. Lead with the **integrated platform / one-AI-that-sees-everything** wedge — point-solution comparison kills the deal.
